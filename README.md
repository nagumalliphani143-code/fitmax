# Fitmax 0.0.1 🚀

Fitmax is a modern, responsive, local-first health and fitness web application inspired by leading health ecosystems like Google Fit and Samsung Health. Built with React/Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, it gives users complete control over their fitness tracking, biometrics, workouts, and nutrition without relying on automated AI fluff or complex servers.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Biometric Settings Engine:** Manage your Name, Age, Height, and Weight. Changes instantly recalculate global metrics like BMI, BMR, and daily caloric targets.
* **Interactive Step Tracker:** Track your daily steps toward a customizable goal with real progress indication, plus an instant **Reset/Minus** button and a **Sensitivity Adjustment** slider in the settings panel.
* **Customizable Weekly Workouts:** Out-of-the-box pre-set routines (Chest & Legs, Abs & Biceps, etc.) with the complete flexibility to add, edit, or customize your own workout schedules and exercises. Includes a set/rep tracker and built-in rest timer.
* **Strictly Manual Nutrition & Macro Logger:** No unwanted AI suggestions or auto-fill guesses. You have total manual control to log custom food items, track Calories, Protein, Carbs, and Fats across Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks.
* **Daily Streaks & Reminders:** Keep yourself accountable with a consecutive active days streak counter, custom health reminder widgets, and a monthly activity calendar.
* **Offline-First Persistence:** All data is saved securely in your browser's `localStorage`.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React, Next.js (App Router), TypeScript
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
* **Storage:** LocalStorage (Offline-first architecture)
